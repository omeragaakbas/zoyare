Is Zoyare a registered Dutch company?

Yes, under KvK number 94498555. Contracts run under Dutch law and invoices are in euros with Dutch BTW. Reverse-charge VAT applies for EU clients outside the Netherlands.

Where is our data stored, and is that AVG-compliant?

On EU infrastructure by default, typically the Amsterdam or Frankfurt regions. You get a processing agreement before any personal data is touched.

Do you work on-site or fully remote?

Mostly remote. For discovery sessions, workshops and go-live moments an on-site day in Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht or The Hague is easy to arrange.

Can you work with our team in Dutch?

Yes. Sessions and daily contact happen in Dutch or English. Code and documentation are written in English so any developer can pick them up.

Why a one-person studio and not an agency?

Rate and directness: you talk to the engineer. The trade-off is capacity. Work that needs five people in parallel fits an agency better, and you hear that in the intake.

What if you become unavailable mid-project?

Code, tests, deployment scripts and a handover file sit in your own repository from day one. Any Dutch developer or agency can take over.

Three questions, an honest range, and a fixed quote within 48 hours of the intake.
