Which processes are worth automating?

Repetitive, rule-based work that takes hours every week: invoicing, reports, data entry between systems, documents. If it follows the same steps each time, automation usually pays for itself quickly.

Will it work with the tools we already use?

Yes. Automation is built around Excel, e-mail, your accounting software and your CRM. Your team keeps working the way it knows.

What if our process changes later?

Rates, templates and recipients are settings you change yourself. A bigger change is a small, quoted adjustment.

How quickly does automation pay for itself?

For a weekly manual process, typically within months. ProAspect's invoicing took several hours every week and now runs automatically.
