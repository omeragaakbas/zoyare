Native or cross-platform?

React Native with Expo: one codebase for iOS and Android. If your app needs full native, for heavy 3D or special hardware, you hear that in the intake.

Do you handle store publication?

Yes, the full process: certificates, store listings, review submission and the questions Apple sends back.

Can you build the back-end too?

Yes. Most apps need accounts, data and notifications, and the API is built alongside the app.

What does maintenance cost after launch?

Apps need updates for new OS versions. Most fixes ship over the air without a store review, and the maintenance setup is agreed before launch.
