Is Zoyare a registered Dutch company?

Yes. Zoyare is registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under KvK number 94498555. Contracts run under Dutch law.

Does one Zoyare engineer do all the work?

Yes, by design. For specific parts such as design or a security audit, selected specialists join, and responsibility stays with Zoyare.

What if Zoyare is unavailable?

Every project ships with documented code, tests and deployment scripts in your own repository, so any developer can take over.

Where does Zoyare work?

Remote first, from the Netherlands. An on-site day in the Randstad or elsewhere in the country is easy to arrange.

Who runs the agents after launch?

Your choice. Zoyare can run and monitor them for you, or hand them to your own team with a runbook.

Where is our data stored?

On EU hosting, under a Dutch contract. Access is agreed per source before anything is connected.

Know the cost before the first call

Three questions, an honest range, and a fixed quote within 48 hours of the intake.
