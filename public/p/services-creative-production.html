Who checks the ads before they go live?

A person on your side, every time. The workflow prepares the set and stops until it is approved.

Which tools make the images and video?

The tools are picked per job and named in the quote, together with their licence and data terms.

Do we own what is made?

Yes. Every file, the prompts and the workflow are handed over to you.

What does an agent cost?

A first agent is priced like a small automation, 5K to 20K euro to build. Running and monitoring it afterwards is a separate line in the quote.

How fast can the first agent go live?

A small automation is live in 2 to 6 weeks after the quote is signed. The timeline is fixed in the quote.

Where is our data stored?

On EU hosting, under a Dutch contract. Access is agreed per source before anything is connected.

Know the cost before the first call

Three questions, an honest range, and a fixed quote within 48 hours of the intake.
