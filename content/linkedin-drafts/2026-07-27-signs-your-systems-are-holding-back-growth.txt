Growth doesn't break businesses. Bad infrastructure does — growth just exposes it faster.

Five signs I see most often when a company has outgrown its systems:

1. Hiring someone doesn't add capacity, just more people doing the same manual workaround
2. One person leaving would break half the operation
3. Reporting takes days and nobody fully trusts the number at the end
4. The same data gets typed into three different systems by hand
5. Doubling revenue more than doubles your back-office hours

None of these show up on a P&L as "systems problem." They show up as overtime, late decisions and stress on the people carrying too much in their heads.

If two or more of these sound familiar, it's worth looking at before the next growth spurt makes it worse: https://zoyare.com/blog/signs-your-systems-are-holding-back-growth
