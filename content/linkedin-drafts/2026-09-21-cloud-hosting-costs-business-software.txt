Your software project has a build cost. It also has a hosting cost you'll pay every month for as long as the software runs.

Most clients don't think about this until the first invoice.

A €40,000 custom build can easily cost €200–€400/month to host — that's €2,400–€4,800/year, compounding over a 5–10 year lifespan. Over five years: up to €24,000 on top of the build.

Three questions worth asking before your developer picks the infrastructure:

→ What does it cost at normal load — and at 10x load?
→ Which country are the servers in? (EU region = GDPR compliant; US-east-1 default = not)
→ Whose name is on the hosting account?

That last one matters more than it sounds. If the account is in your developer's name, you don't really control your own application — you're one difficult conversation away from losing access to everything.

For Dutch and EU businesses: verify the EU region explicitly. It's not the default in most tutorials or most developers' habits.
