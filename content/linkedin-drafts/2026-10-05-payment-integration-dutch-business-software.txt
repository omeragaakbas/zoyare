Most Dutch software projects underestimate payment integration.

Not because iDEAL or Stripe is hard to use — the APIs are well-documented. But payments have more edge cases than almost any other integration.

A webhook arrives twice. A card expires mid-subscription. A user disputes a charge three weeks after delivery. The happy path is easy. Everything around it takes real engineering.

A few things I see clients get wrong:

1. Skipping iDEAL. It's 70% of Dutch online transactions. Launching without it is a conversion disaster.

2. Budgeting only for the happy path. Webhook idempotency, refund flows, state management — that's 3–4x the work of just "calling the API."

3. Ignoring PSD2/SCA. Recurring card charges need to be flagged correctly as merchant-initiated or you'll see soft declines. This isn't automatic.

4. Not modelling transaction fees upfront. Mollie's flat €0.29 per iDEAL transaction vs. a percentage-based provider makes a real difference at volume.

Payment integration in custom software typically runs €4,000–€25,000 depending on scope — on top of the main build. Worth knowing before you budget the project.
