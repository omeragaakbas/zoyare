88% of spreadsheets contain errors. And yet, most businesses I work with run their core operations on Excel.

Not because they want to. Because it started small and grew into a 47-tab monster that nobody dares touch.

Five signs you've outgrown Excel:
- Multiple people fight over the same file
- Someone copies data between sheets for hours every week
- The file takes 30+ seconds to open
- Business logic lives in formulas only one person understands
- Your weekly report is always "slightly off"

The cost of staying too long? A process that takes 4 hours/week in Excel often takes 10 minutes in a proper system. That's €10,000+ per year in labor — for one process.

But not every spreadsheet needs replacing. The goal is to stop using Excel for things it was never designed to do.
