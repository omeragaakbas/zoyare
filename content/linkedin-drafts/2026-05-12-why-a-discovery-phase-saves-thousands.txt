Most software projects don't fail because of bad code. They fail because the wrong thing gets built.

I've seen the same pattern repeat: a company writes a clear brief, development starts fast, and six weeks in the first demo lands with "this is what we asked for, but not what we meant."

The fix? Two to three weeks of discovery before writing any code. Talk to actual users. Map the real workflow. Define what v1 actually needs.

Cost: €2,500–€5,000 (3–5% of a typical project budget).
What it prevents: €10,000–€15,000 in rework and scope creep.

That's a 3–5x return before a single feature is built.
