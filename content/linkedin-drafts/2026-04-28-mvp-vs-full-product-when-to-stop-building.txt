Most software projects fail not because the idea is bad — but because v1 tries to do too much.

I see it constantly: a €50K+ budget spent on features that nobody ends up using. Meanwhile the core problem still isn't solved properly.

The fix? Start with an MVP that does one thing well. Ship it. Get feedback. Then invest where it actually matters.

Some numbers from my own practice:
→ MVP cost: €15K–€35K (6–10 weeks)
→ Full product: €50K–€120K (4–8 months)
→ ~60% of features in a full build are rarely or never used

For ProAspect, our invoicing tool, the MVP was intentionally narrow: CSV upload → auto-generated invoices → PDF export. Replaced 4+ hours of manual work per week from day one. Cost: under €10K.

The client portal and email integration? Those came in v2 — after we confirmed the core actually worked.

Stop building features. Start solving problems.
