The European Accessibility Act took effect in June 2025.

Most Dutch business owners assume it only applies to government websites. It doesn't.

If you run a customer-facing website or app in e-commerce, financial services, or transport — you have a legal obligation to meet WCAG 2.1 AA. New services must already comply. Existing services have until June 28, 2030.

What I keep seeing: businesses that commission new software without mentioning accessibility in the brief. The developer builds to the brief. No one checks contrast ratios or keyboard navigation. The product launches.

Retrofitting accessibility after the fact costs 30–50% of the original front-end work. Building it in from the start: 5–15% extra.

A few things worth knowing before you commission or audit a system:

→ Businesses with < 10 staff and < €2M turnover are exempt (microenterprise carve-out)
→ Internal tools are generally outside the EAA's scope
→ Automated audits catch ~40% of issues — manual testing catches the rest
→ A "does this work with only a keyboard" test is a 10-minute check you can do yourself today
