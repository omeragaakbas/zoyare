"How many developers do we need?" is the wrong first question.

Team size isn't an input you choose. It's an output of what the work looks like.

Two things people underestimate:

1. On most business software the bottleneck isn't typing, it's decisions. Which spreadsheet is the source of truth? Who signs off? Those answers come from the client side. If a decision takes four days, a second developer just means two people waiting.

2. Coordination isn't free. Two people have one relationship to maintain. Four have six. Eight have twenty-eight. One developer to four isn't 4x output - realistically 2-3x.

What one senior developer delivers full time:
→ Internal tool or portal: 2-4 months
→ Integration between two systems: 3-8 weeks
→ Line-of-business app replacing a spreadsheet process: 4-6 months

Staff a team when the deadline genuinely can't move, the work splits cleanly, or it needs skills one person can't credibly hold. "It's important" isn't on that list - important and large aren't the same thing.
