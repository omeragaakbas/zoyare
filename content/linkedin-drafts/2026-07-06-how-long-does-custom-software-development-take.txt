"How long will it take?" is the second question in every first call — right after cost. And the answers online are uselessly vague.

So, real numbers for typical business software:

→ API integration between two systems: 2–6 weeks
→ Internal tool or MVP: 6–12 weeks
→ Customer-facing app or portal: 3–6 months
→ Replacing a legacy system: 4–9 months

But here's the part nobody tells you: the timeline is barely about how fast anyone types. Four things dominate it:

1. Scope clarity — decisions made upfront vs. a new discussion per screen
2. Integrations — one undocumented legacy API costs more time than a month of coding
3. Your feedback speed — reviews that sit in an inbox for 3 weeks turn a 6-week project into a 4-month one, with zero extra work done
4. How many people can say "no" — one decision-maker is fast, a monthly steering committee is not

The good news: three of those four are under YOUR control.
