Your company needs custom software. The first question isn't what to build — it's who builds it.

Hiring a mid-level developer in NL: €75,000–€100,000/year fully loaded. Add 2–4 months of recruitment and 2–3 months of ramp-up before any real output.

Outsourcing to a studio: €32,000–€104,000 for the project. Full team from day one. Production starts this month, not in six months.

Neither option is always better. But the decision becomes clear when you ask:

- Is this a project (has an end date) or a program (runs forever)?
- Do you need one skill set or four?
- What happens after launch — daily changes or monthly maintenance?
- Can you actually retain technical talent long-term?

Most growing companies end up hybrid: outsource v1, hire once the product is proven, keep the studio for specialized work.
