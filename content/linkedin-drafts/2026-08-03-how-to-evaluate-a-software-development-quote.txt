Got three quotes for the same software project? €18k, €42k, €65k?

That's normal. It doesn't mean two of them are wrong — it usually means the three quotes aren't pricing the same thing, even though they look like they are.

Before comparing numbers, compare:
→ Is the scope specific, or one vague sentence?
→ Does the contract model (fixed price vs. hourly) match how uncertain the work actually is?
→ Is there a discovery phase, or straight into building on assumptions?
→ Do you own the code, domain and credentials when it's done?
→ What's covered after launch — and what isn't?

The cheapest number on the page is sometimes the best deal. Sometimes it's a studio that hasn't found the complexity yet, or plans to recover it through change requests later.
