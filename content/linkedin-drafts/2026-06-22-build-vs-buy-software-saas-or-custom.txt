"Should we buy a SaaS tool or build something custom?"

It's the most common question I get — and the honest answer is almost never "build."

For 80% of business processes, off-the-shelf software wins. It's cheaper, it's live next week, and someone else maintains it. If your problem is generic — accounting, CRM, project management — buying is the right call, and a developer who tells you otherwise is selling hours.

So when does custom actually win?

→ The process is your competitive edge, not a commodity. Standardising it to fit a SaaS box throws away the thing that makes you you.

→ The subscription math flips. €2,500/month across 40 seats is €30k/year — forever. A €40k custom tool can pay back in well under two years.

→ You're already paying in glue. Three tools that don't talk, an "ops" hire whose real job is copy-pasting between them. That's a hidden custom-software bill you're paying in salary.

The trap isn't buying or building. It's buying five tools that each solve 80% of a problem, then drowning in the gaps between them.
